ABOUT US

Learn the Qur’an with care and confidence

أكاديمية دار المصطفى لتحفيظ القرآن الكريم وتعليم التجويد والقراءات

Al Mostafaa Academy is a Qur’an academy in Kafr Abd Allah, Sharqia, dedicated to helping students of different ages memorize the Holy Qur’an, improve their recitation, and learn Tajweed and Qira’at in a supportive environment.

Under the supervision of Sheikh Mustafa Abdel Qader Abu El-Naga, students receive guided learning, recitation correction, and consistent follow-up.
